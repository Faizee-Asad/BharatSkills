# Marriage Biodata Maker

For Indian families preparing a marriage biodata for their son or daughter — to share
with relatives, marriage bureaus, community groups, or on WhatsApp.

## When to use this skill
- "Shadi ke liye biodata banana hai" / "Biodata format chahiye"
- "Ladke ka biodata Hindi me banao" / "Rishta biodata kaise likhe?"
- "Kundali wali details biodata me kaise daalein?"
- "WhatsApp par bhejne ke liye chhota biodata format"

## What you need from the user
Ask only what the family wants to include. Typical details:
- Full name, date of birth, time and place of birth (only if kundali details are wanted)
- Height, gotra / religion / community (as the family states it)
- Education and occupation (with workplace city; income only if the family wants it)
- Father's and mother's name and occupation, siblings (married/unmarried)
- Current city and native place
- Contact: usually a parent/guardian's mobile number
- Expectations from the match (age, education, city) — optional

A biodata gets forwarded far beyond the first receiver. Advise the family:
- Do NOT print full home address, Aadhaar, or any ID number on it.
- One contact number (parent/guardian) is enough; the candidate's personal number
  and social-media IDs can be shared later, directly.
- A photo is commonly attached separately — it is the family's choice, never a must
  in the text format itself.

## Choose the format the family follows

### 1. Traditional Hindu format (Hindi or English)
Usually starts with an invocation (e.g. ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥) and has two blocks:
- **Janm details (for kundali matching):** janm tithi, samay, sthan, gotra, rashi,
  nakshatra, and manglik status — include exactly what the family believes and
  follows; if they don't follow kundali matching, skip the whole block.
- **Parichay details:** education, occupation, family, contact.

### 2. Muslim format (English, Urdu or Hinglish)
No kundali block. Sections: personal details (name, DOB, height), deen/education,
profession, family details (parents, siblings), residence, expectations, and contact
through a parent/guardian or wali. Families often begin with Bismillah at the top —
include it if the family wants it.

### 3. Christian / Sikh / other formats
Follow the family's own community style: personal, family, education, occupation,
church/denomination details only if the family includes them. When unsure, use the
modern format below and add what the family asks for.

### 4. Modern short format (WhatsApp-friendly)
One screen, no religious blocks: name, age, height, education, job + city, family in
one line, native place, contact. Best for first sharing in groups; the detailed
biodata follows when a family shows interest.

## Workflow
1. Ask whose biodata (son/daughter), which format above, and Hindi or English.
2. Collect the details in the order of the chosen template — don't ask for anything
   the template doesn't use.
3. Draft the biodata with placeholders for anything not yet given, and show it back
   for correction — names, dates and gotra spellings must be checked by the family.
4. Suggest the sharing order: short format first on WhatsApp/groups; full biodata
   only to interested families.

गोत्र:           <गोत्र>        राशि: <राशि>      नक्षत्र: <नक्षत्र>
मांगलिक:        <हाँ / नहीं / आंशिक>
ऊँचाई:          <फीट-इंच>

शिक्षा:          <डिग्री, संस्थान>

पिता:           <नाम> — <व्यवसाय>

### Muslim format (English)
```
Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim

        BIODATA FOR MARRIAGE

Name:            <Full Name>
Date of Birth:   <DD-MM-YYYY>          Height: <ft-in>
Education:       <Degree, Institution>
Profession:      <Job/Business, City>  (Income: <if family wishes>)

Father:          <Name> — <Occupation>
Mother:          <Name> — <Occupation>
Siblings:        <Details>
Residence:       <City, State>         Native Place: <Place>

Expectations:    <Age, education, city — if any>
Contact:         <Parent/Guardian Name> — <Mobile>
```

### Modern short format (WhatsApp)
```
🌸 Biodata — <Name> 🌸
Age: <age> | Height: <ft-in> | <City>
Education: <degree>
Work: <job/business, city>
Family: Father — <occupation>, Mother — <occupation>, <siblings in one line>
Native place: <place>
Contact: <Parent name> — <mobile>
```

## Rules & common mistakes
- ❌ Hiding or inflating facts — income doubled, age reduced, a previous marriage or
  a health condition hidden. Families verify, and a match broken later hurts far
  more than honesty at the start.
- ❌ Printing full address / Aadhaar / candidate's personal number on a document
  that will be forwarded in groups.
- ❌ Spelling errors in name, gotra, or birth details — for kundali matching, one
  wrong digit in birth time changes the chart. Ask the family to double-check.
- ❌ Adding kundali/manglik blocks for a family that doesn't follow them — or
  removing them for a family that does. The family's practice decides, not the format.
- ✅ Keep one master copy with the family and edit copies from it — five versions
  with five different details is a common family headache.
- ✅ This skill only prepares the document. Decisions about a match belong entirely
  to the families — never advise for or against a specific match.

# School Fee Reminder

For school and college office staff in India who must collect fees without damaging
the relationship with parents. Fees are sensitive — a parent may be late because of
a genuine problem. Every template here is firm on facts and soft on tone.

## When to use this skill
- "Parents ko fees reminder bhejna hai" (WhatsApp / SMS / notice)
- First instalment / second instalment due notices
- "Receipt mil gayi hai, confirm karna hai"
- Final notice before exam / report-card related deadlines — worded carefully and legally safe

## What you need from the user
- School/college name
- Student class/section (use placeholders in templates — never put a real student's full record in an example)
- Fee amount (₹), instalment number, due date
- Payment options the school accepts (UPI / bank / cash at office) and office timings
- Stage: 1st notice, follow-up, or final notice
- Language: English / Hindi / Marathi

Privacy rule: reminders go to the parent of that student only. Never share one
family's dues with another parent, a class group, or a public list. A class
WhatsApp group must never be used to name students with pending fees.

## Tone ladder

| Stage | When | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Notice | Before / on due date | Informative, helpful |
| 2. Gentle follow-up | 7–15 days after due date | Kind, offers instalment/help |
| 3. Final notice | 30+ days | Formal, states the consequence the school's policy actually has |

## Templates

### Stage 1 — Fee notice (English, WhatsApp/SMS)
```
Namaste, <School Name> se suchna:
<Student Name> (Class <Class>) ki <Instalment> fees ₹<Amount> hai,
 antim tarikh <Due Date> hai.
Payment: UPI <School UPI ID> / office me <Office Timings>.
Receipt payment ke baad milegi. Dhanyavaad.
```

### Stage 1 — Hindi
```
सूचना — <School Name>
प्रिय अभिभावक, <Student Name> (कक्षा <Class>) की <Instalment> फीस
₹<Amount> है, अंतिम तिथि <Due Date> है।
भुगतान UPI <School UPI ID> द्वारा या कार्यालय समय <Office Timings> में करें।
धन्यवाद।
```

### Stage 1 — Marathi
```
सूचना — <School Name>
प्रिय पालक, <Student Name> (इयत्ता <Class>) ची <Instalment> फी
₹<Amount> असून शेवटची तारीख <Due Date> आहे.
पेमेंट UPI <School UPI ID> ने किंवा कार्यालयीन वेळेत <Office Timings> करा.
धन्यवाद.
```

### Stage 2 — Gentle follow-up (English)
```
Namaste <Parent Name> ji, <School Name> se ek vinamra reminder —
<Student Name> (Class <Class>) ki ₹<Amount> fees abhi baki hai.
Koi dikkat ho to office me aa kar instalment ki baat kar sakte hain,
hum poori madad karenge. — <School Name> Office
```

### Stage 3 — Final notice (formal, printed/letter)
```
FINAL FEE NOTICE — <School Name>
Date: <Date>
To, The Parent of <Student Name>, Class <Class>
This is to inform you that school fees of ₹<Amount> for <Instalment/Period>,
due on <Due Date>, are still pending despite earlier reminders.
You are requested to clear the dues by <Final Date> at the school office
(<Office Timings>) or via UPI (<School UPI ID>).
If there is any difficulty, please meet the office in person before <Final Date>
so we can help you with an instalment plan.
— Principal / Office, <School Name>
```
Only state a consequence (e.g. regarding exams or documents) if it is the school's
actual written policy and permitted under the applicable state/board rules —
if unsure, leave the consequence out and mark `verify before use`.

### Receipt confirmation (after payment)
```
Namaste, <Student Name> (Class <Class>) ki fees ₹<Amount> mil gayi hai ✅
Receipt No: <Receipt No.>, Tarikh: <Date>. Dhanyavaad! — <School Name>
```

## Rules & common mistakes
- ❌ Never announce a child's pending fees in a class group, assembly or public list — it humiliates the family and can create legal trouble.
- ❌ Never threaten to stop a child's education in a first or second reminder.
- ❌ Don't send reminders on festival days or late at night.
- ✅ Always offer a human door: "office me aa kar baat karein" — many cases resolve with one instalment conversation.
- ✅ Keep a simple record: date reminder sent, stage, and response — it protects the school later.
- ✅ Receipt confirmation the same day builds trust and reduces office visits.

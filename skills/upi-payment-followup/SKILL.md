# UPI Payment Follow-up

For anyone in India waiting to be paid — a freelancer, shopkeeper, tutor, agency or
service provider. Produces a ready-to-send reminder in the right tone for how
overdue the payment is, with a UPI payment line the customer can act on at once.

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India's instant bank-to-bank payment system —
customers pay by scanning a QR code or using a UPI ID like `name@bank` in apps
such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm.

## When to use this skill
- "Payment reminder bhejna hai" / "Write a follow-up for pending payment"
- "Client ne kaam le liya, payment atka hai"
- "Polite reminder for ₹<amount> UPI payment"

## What you need from the user
- Customer/client first name
- Amount (₹) and what it is for (invoice no. / work / order)
- Due date and how many days overdue it is
- Your UPI ID or "QR bhej dunga" — and your business name as it appears on UPI
- Relationship: new customer, regular, or long-term (controls how firm we can be)

Never ask for or include bank account numbers, passwords or OTPs. A UPI ID / QR
is enough for someone to pay you — and a real payment request never asks the
receiver to share an OTP or PIN. If a draft ever does, remove it.

## Tone ladder — pick by days overdue

| Stage | When | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Gentle nudge | On due date / 1–3 days | Friendly, assumes they forgot |
| 2. Clear reminder | 4–10 days | Polite but specific — amount, date, UPI ID |
| 3. Firm follow-up | 11–30 days | Short, serious, asks for a payment date |
| 4. Final notice | 30+ days | Formal, states next step (pause work / late fee if agreed) |

Never threaten, abuse or shame. If the user wants a stage-4 message, keep it
factual and professional — it may be read by others later.

## Templates

### Stage 1 — Gentle (WhatsApp, Hinglish)
```
Namaste <Name> ji, ek chhota sa reminder — <work/order> ke ₹<Amount> ka payment
aaj due tha. Jab time mile to UPI kar dijiyega: <Your UPI ID>
QR bhi bhej raha hu. Dhanyavaad! 🙏
```

### Stage 2 — Clear reminder (English)
```
Hi <Name>, following up on invoice <Invoice No.> of ₹<Amount> for <work>,
due on <Due Date>. You can pay instantly via UPI: <Your UPI ID> (<Business Name>).
Please share the screenshot/UTR once done so I can close it from my side. Thanks!
```

### Stage 2 — Hindi
```
Namaste <Name> ji, <work> ke ₹<Amount> (Bill No. <Invoice No.>, tarikh <Due Date>)
ka bhugtan abhi baki hai. Aap UPI se aasani se bhej sakte hain: <Your UPI ID>
Bhugtan ke baad screenshot bhej dijiyega. Dhanyavaad.
```

### Stage 3 — Firm (English)
```
Hi <Name>, this is my third follow-up for ₹<Amount> (invoice <Invoice No.>,
due <Due Date>, now <N> days overdue). Please complete the payment via UPI
(<Your UPI ID>) by <New Date>, or tell me a confirmed date today.
I have paused further work until this is cleared.
```

### Stage 4 — Final notice (formal, email-ready)
```
Subject: Final reminder — ₹<Amount> overdue (Invoice <Invoice No.>)

Dear <Name>,
Despite earlier reminders, ₹<Amount> for <work> (due <Due Date>) remains unpaid
as of today. Please pay via UPI (<Your UPI ID>) within 3 days of this message.
Failing that, I will <agreed next step — e.g. apply the late fee stated in our
agreement / hand the matter to my accountant for formal recovery>.
If you have already paid, please share the UTR number and ignore this notice.
— <Your Name>, <Business Name>
```

## Rules & common mistakes
- ✅ Always include: amount, what it's for, and the UPI ID — a reminder without a way to pay doesn't get paid.
- ✅ One reminder, one channel. Don't send the same message on WhatsApp, SMS and email on the same day.
- ✅ Keep proof: ask for screenshot/UTR after payment, and keep your messages polite and saved.
- ❌ Never ask the payer for their UPI PIN, OTP or bank login — that's how fraud works, and it destroys trust.
- ❌ Don't add a late fee that was never agreed — mention it only if it was in the original quote/agreement.
- ❌ Don't message at odd hours; business hours (9am–8pm IST) get better responses.

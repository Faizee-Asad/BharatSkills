# School Admission Helper

For school and college office staff who answer admission enquiries every new session —
and for parents who want to know the process and documents before visiting.

> Fees, dates, seats and scholarships differ for every school and change every
> session. This skill gives the structure and messages only — every figure in it is
> a placeholder that the school must fill from its own official fee chart and
> admission notice. Never quote a fee or a last date from memory.

## When to use this skill
- "Admission ki enquiry aayi hai, reply kya bheju?" (a parent asked — what do I reply?)
- "Admission ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?" — from staff or from a parent
- "Enquiry wale parents ko follow-up kaise karu?" (they asked but never visited)
- "Admission confirm ho gaya — parent ko message banana hai"

## What you need (from the school, before replying to anyone)
- Classes with admission open, and the admission window (start–last date)
- Fee chart for this session (admission fee + term/monthly fee) — official copy
- Documents list the school actually asks for
- Transport routes available, school timings, and visit timings for parents
- Any scholarship/concession the management has *approved in writing*

## Standard document checklist (parents ke liye)
Schools commonly ask for — the school's own list is final:
- Student's birth certificate copy (for Nursery–Class 1 entry) / previous class
  marksheet and Transfer Certificate (TC) for higher classes
- Aadhaar copies of student and parents (as ID/address proof, where the school asks)
- Passport-size photos of the student (usually 2–4) and one family photo if asked
- Address proof (any one: Aadhaar, electricity bill, rent agreement)
- Caste/category certificate copy — only if applying under a category/quota
  (e.g. RTE or state schemes; the exact rules vary by state — `verify before use`)

Handling note for staff: these are children's personal documents. Take only the
copies your list needs, keep them in the admission file, and never forward a child's
documents or photos on personal WhatsApp or social media.

## Workflow — for the school office

### 1. Reply to the enquiry the same day
A parent who asks 5 schools joins the one that replies first and clearest.
First reply must contain: classes open, documents, visit timing, and one contact
number — template below.

### 2. Invite for a campus visit
Most admissions close at the visit, not on the phone. Offer 2–3 specific time slots
instead of "kabhi bhi aa jaiye".

### 3. Follow up politely (maximum 3 touches)
- Day 2–3: short reminder with visit slots.
- Day 6–7: "seats are filling for <class>" — only if that is actually true.
- After that, stop. A parent who is pushed daily joins another school.

### 4. On admission: confirm in writing
Send the confirmation message (template below) with: class, section (if allotted),
fee amount paid + receipt number, next fee date, and the first day of school /
orientation date if fixed.

### 5. Keep a simple enquiry register
Name, class sought, mobile, source (walk-in / call / WhatsApp / reference), status
(visited / admitted / not interested). One notebook or one spreadsheet — this
register is next session's starting list and shows which source brings admissions.

## Templates

### First enquiry reply (WhatsApp, Hinglish)
```
Namaste 🙏 <School Name> me sampark karne ke liye dhanyavaad.
Session <2026–27> ke liye <Nursery se Class 12 tak> admissions khule hain.
📄 Documents: janm praman patra / pichli class ki marksheet + TC, Aadhaar copy, 4 photo
💰 Fees ki puri jaankari office se milegi — aap <samay, jaise subah 9–1> ke beech aakar mil sakte hain.
📍 <School address> | 📞 <Office number>
Aap kis class ke liye admission chahte hain?
```

### Follow-up (Day 2–3)
```
Namaste <Parent Name> ji, <School Name> se.
Aapne <Class> admission ke liye pucha tha — kal <time slots> par school visit ke liye uplabdh hain.
Bacche ke saath aaiye, campus aur teachers se milkar nirnay lena aasan hoga. 🙏
```

### Admission confirmation
```
🎉 Badhai ho! <Student Name> ka admission <School Name>, Class <X>, me pakka ho gaya hai.
Receipt No: <no.> | Jama Fees: ₹<amount> | Agli fees ki tithi: <date>
School khulne ki tithi: <date>
Kisi jaankari ke liye office: <number>. Dhanyavaad! 🙏
```

### For parents — visit par jaane se pehle (checklist message schools bhej sakte hain)
```
Admission visit ke liye ye saath laayein:
✅ Pichli class ki marksheet + TC (Class 2 se upar)
✅ Janm praman patra ki copy (chhoti class ke liye)
✅ Aadhaar copy — baccha + mata-pita
✅ 4 passport photo
✅ Fees ke liye <cash/UPI/cheque — jo school leta hai>
```

## Rules & common mistakes
- ❌ Quoting fees or a "last date" that the office never approved — one wrong figure
  on WhatsApp becomes a screenshot argument later. Placeholders only, filled from
  the official chart.
- ❌ Promising a seat, a section, transport on a route, or a scholarship that
  management has not approved.
- ❌ Saying "seats full hone wale hain" when they are not — pressure tactics break
  the trust schools run on.
- ❌ Sharing enquiry lists, children's documents or photos outside the office.
- ❌ Asking parents for documents on WhatsApp photos when the school needs physical
  copies — say clearly what to bring in hand.
- ✅ Reply the same day, in the parent's language where possible (Hindi/Marathi/
  regional) — for most Indian parents, that respect decides the school.
- ✅ This skill covers communication and process only. RTE/state-quota admissions
  follow government rules and dates — check the current official notice and mark
  `verify before use`.

# Changelog

All notable changes to BharatSkills are documented here.
Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/), versions follow SemVer.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `salary-slip-india` — Indian salary slip format and checking: PF/ESI/Professional Tax deductions, paid-days/LOP calculation, worked examples.
- `hindi-typing-helper` — Kruti Dev ↔ Unicode conversion workflow, font/layout guidance, government Hindi typing-exam preparation.
- `resume-fresher-india` — fresher resume builder for Indian job portals: structure, role-specific objective, projects-over-experience approach, ATS-friendly formatting.
- `festival-offer-planner` — festival offer and campaign planning for Indian businesses: festival calendar, T-minus timeline, honest offer design, Diwali/Eid/Rakhi WhatsApp templates.
- `marriage-biodata-maker` — marriage biodata formats for Indian families: traditional Hindu (with kundali block), Muslim, and modern WhatsApp-short formats, with privacy guidance.
- `school-admission-helper` — school admission enquiry handling: same-day parent replies, document checklist, 3-touch follow-up, confirmation messages.
- `loan-emi-helper` — loan and EMI explainer for Indian borrowers: EMI maths with worked examples, reducing vs flat rate trap, bank vs NBFC total-cost comparison, prepayment rules.
- `traffic-challan-helper` — traffic e-challan guide: verify/pay only on the official Parivahan portal, contesting a wrong challan (grievance / Virtual Court / Lok Adalat), and spotting fake challan SMS scams.
- `freelancer-proposal-india` — proposal and quotation builder for Indian freelancers: scope-first pricing, 50/30/20 milestone payments, revision caps, GST line, worked examples and a late-payment follow-up template.
- `shopkeeper-ledger` — daily ledger format for Indian shops: cash/UPI/udhaar sales columns, closing cash tally with short/excess tracking, udhaar khata with 0–7/8–30/30+ day ageing, weekly summary and a polite Hinglish recovery reminder.
